Our story

In Play Tips started with a simple frustration: too many tipsters shout about their wins and quietly bury the losses. We wanted to do the opposite — post every tip before the event, settle it honestly afterwards, and let the numbers do the talking across a full season.

Today we cover the sports we know best — NRL, AFL, NBA, NFL, soccer and racing — with staking guidance that scales to any bankroll and a results ledger that's open to every member.
